Verified mechanic

The verified loop at a glance

The official listing gives a clear set of connected systems: clicks add Punch, Punch is used against ASMR walls, Wins support stronger gloves, and training can unlock powerful multipliers.

  • Click or tap to add Punch.
  • Punch through available ASMR walls.
  • Claim Wins and inspect stronger gloves.
  • Use current multiplier and rebirth prompts to grow faster.
Verified mechanic

Punch is the input

The only control relationship confirmed by the official source is simple: every click equals +1 Punch. The guide does not assume keyboard shortcuts, auto-click features, or exact rate limits.

Verified mechanic

Walls turn Punch into forward progress

Walls are the central obstacle named by the game description, while Wins are a stated reward and progression resource. Exact wall requirements and Win amounts can change or remain undocumented, so read them in the live game.

Verified mechanic

Gloves, multipliers, trails, and rebirth shape growth

Stronger gloves, powerful multipliers, speed-boosting trails, and rebirth are all named in the official description. The source does not provide their prices, tiers, or reset rules.

Guide strategy

Find the slowest part of your loop

When progress feels slow, ask which step is holding you back: building Punch, clearing the available wall, reaching a glove requirement, or moving between objectives. Change one choice at a time and compare the result.
