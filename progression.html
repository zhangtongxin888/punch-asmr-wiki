Verified mechanic

Know the systems that are actually confirmed

The verified progression systems are stronger gloves, powerful multipliers, speed-focused trails, leaderboards, and rebirth. The official description also names Wins as part of unlocking stronger gloves.

Guide strategy

Early route: make the base loop readable

Before optimizing, understand how your current Punch translates into wall progress and how the game presents Wins. This gives you a baseline for judging the upgrades the live interface offers.

  • Observe your current Punch gain.
  • Check an available wall requirement.
  • Inspect glove and multiplier prompts after a completed loop.
Guide strategy

Middle route: compare power and movement needs

Gloves and multipliers relate to power growth, while trails are described as helping you run faster. Compare the problem you are currently experiencing before choosing where to focus.

Keep in mind

This is a decision framework, not a claim that one upgrade category is always best.

Verification note

Treat rebirth as a deliberate reset decision

Rebirth is officially described as helping you grow faster, but the verified source does not document its requirement, multiplier, or reset effects. Read the current confirmation screen in full before committing.

Guide strategy

Use leaderboards as a long-term signal

Climbing leaderboards is part of the game's stated appeal. For most new players, it is more useful as a later benchmark than as the first objective of a session.
